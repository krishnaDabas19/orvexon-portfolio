About Me

KRISHNA DABAS

I'm Krishna Dabas, founder of Orvexon Studio and a graphic designer currently pursuing B.Tech in Computer Science with specialization in AI & ML at Lovely Professional University, Punjab.

My interest lies in branding, visual storytelling, social media design, and modern digital aesthetics. I enjoy creating projects that combine creativity with structure — focusing on clean layouts, typography, and consistent visual systems.

Through Orvexon Studio, I work on concept branding projects, campaign visuals, poster designs, and portfolio experiences that reflect a modern and minimal creative approach. Alongside design, my technical background helps me understand digital experiences from both a visual and implementation perspective.

I'm constantly exploring new ways to improve my creative process, develop stronger visual identities, and build projects that feel intentional, polished, and professionally presented.

"I believe good design is not just about visuals — it's about clarity, consistency, and creating experiences that leave a lasting impression."
